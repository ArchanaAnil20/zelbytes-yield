Capstone Reflection

This project helped me understand the complete data science lifecycle from data cleaning to deployment. I learned how to perform exploratory data analysis, train machine learning models, compare performance metrics, and deploy a Streamlit application to the cloud.

The three most valuable skills I learned were data preprocessing, model evaluation, and cloud deployment. I also gained experience in monitoring, logging, and technical documentation.

Areas for future improvement include working with larger real-world datasets and implementing automated retraining pipelines. Overall, this project strengthened my confidence in building end-to-end data science solutions.
